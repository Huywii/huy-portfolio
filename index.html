How sales tax software works: calculating tax and managing tax-exempt customers

An interactive 3D model of the software businesses use to charge the right sales tax. Follow a sales invoice through a tax engine as it checks the address, applies the rules, and calculates the tax. Then follow a tax-exemption certificate, the form a customer such as a reseller provides to buy without paying sales tax, through exemption certificate management (ECM) as it is collected, validated, and stored. Each step explains what happens and shows sample data.
